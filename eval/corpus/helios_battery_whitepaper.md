# Helios S2 Sodium-Ion Cell: Technical Whitepaper

*Helios Energy Labs, March 2026 (fictional document for evaluation)*

## Overview

The Helios S2 is a sodium-ion battery cell designed for grid storage and light electric
vehicles. It contains no lithium, cobalt or nickel, which removes the most supply-constrained
materials from the bill of materials.

## Performance

- **Energy density:** 165 Wh/kg at the cell level, close to the 170 Wh/kg of typical LFP cells.
- **Cycle life:** 4,000 full charge cycles before capacity falls to 80 percent of its original value.
- **Fast charging:** 10 to 80 percent state of charge in 18 minutes at 25 °C.
- **Operating temperature:** -30 °C to 60 °C, with 88 percent of capacity retained at -20 °C.

## Cost

Current production cost is 78 USD per kWh. Helios targets 62 USD per kWh by 2027 once the
second production line in Aarhus, Denmark reaches full output.

## Safety

The S2 passed nail-penetration and overcharge tests without thermal runaway. Cells can be
shipped at 0 percent charge, which simplifies transport regulations.
