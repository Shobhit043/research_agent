Remote Work Policy (fictional document for evaluation)
Effective 1 January 2026

1. Eligibility
Employees may work remotely after completing their 90-day probation period.
Roles that require on-site equipment, such as lab technicians, are excluded.

2. Schedule
Eligible employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.
Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time zone; employees must be
reachable during core hours.

3. Home office support
New remote workers receive a one-time home office stipend of 600 USD, plus 40 USD per
month towards internet costs.

4. Equipment
Company laptops and monitors remain company property and must be returned within
14 days of leaving the company.

5. Working abroad
Employees may work from another country for up to 30 days per calendar year, with prior
written approval from HR.
